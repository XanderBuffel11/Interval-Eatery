01 — About

A quiet pause between everything else.

Interval is a small café on Hinemoa Street in Rotorua. We keep things simple: a short menu cooked properly, coffee poured with care, and a room where nobody is in a hurry.

Pull up a chair for breakfast, grab a flat white on the way through, or have us bring it to you.
